Privacy Policy

How Iterate Social handles data

This Privacy Policy explains how Iterate Social collects, uses, stores, and protects information when people visit our website, join our waitlist, or connect third-party services such as LinkedIn for content publishing and analytics workflows.

Effective date: March 12, 2026 Product: Iterate Social Contact: hello@iterate.social

Who we are

Iterate Social is a software product that helps individuals and teams plan, generate, publish, and review social content. This policy applies to the Iterate Social website, waitlist, and connected workflows used to publish posts and review post performance.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide directly, such as your email address when joining the waitlist or contacting us.
  • Account and connection data needed to operate the product, such as authorized LinkedIn account identifiers and access tokens.
  • Content and publishing data, such as draft text, hashtags, publish timestamps, and post identifiers created through the app.
  • Performance data returned by connected platforms, such as likes, comments, and other metrics made available by the platform.
  • Basic technical data, such as browser type, device information, and diagnostic logs used for security and product reliability.

How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve Iterate Social.
  • To authenticate users and maintain secure connections with integrated third-party platforms.
  • To publish content that a user has explicitly requested the app to publish.
  • To retrieve and display engagement metrics for posts when the connected platform makes those metrics available.
  • To communicate with users about product updates, support requests, and operational notices.
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, unauthorized access, and technical issues.

LinkedIn data

When a user connects a LinkedIn account, Iterate Social may store and process the minimum information needed to perform the requested action, such as account identifiers, access tokens, post identifiers, publish timestamps, and post engagement data returned by LinkedIn APIs.

Iterate Social does not sell LinkedIn data. We use LinkedIn data only to support user-requested publishing, analytics, and product functionality.

Legal bases and purpose limitation

We process information to deliver the services requested by users, to operate our business, to comply with legal obligations, and, where appropriate, based on consent. We limit use of connected platform data to the purposes described in this policy.

Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information only in limited situations, including:

  • With infrastructure and service providers that help us operate the product, such as hosting, database, analytics, and email providers.
  • With integrated platforms when a user initiates an action, such as publishing a post to LinkedIn.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Access tokens and connected account data may be deleted or revoked when no longer needed.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect data in line with industry practice.

Your choices and rights

  • You may choose not to provide certain information, though some features may then be unavailable.
  • You may revoke third-party platform access from the platform account settings or by contacting us.
  • You may request access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable law and technical limitations.

Children's privacy

Iterate Social is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date on this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the product or by other appropriate means.
