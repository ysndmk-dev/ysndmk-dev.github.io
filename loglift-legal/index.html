LogLift Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 23, 2026

LogLift is an offline-first gym workout tracker. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app processes, how that information is used, and the choices you have when using LogLift.

1. Information LogLift Stores

LogLift is designed to work primarily on your device. Depending on how you use the app, LogLift may store:

  • Workout data such as exercises, sets, reps, weight, workout duration, and personal records
  • Routine data such as saved workout templates and exercise order
  • App settings such as weight unit, rest timer preferences, sound preferences, and notification preferences
  • Imported or exported workout files that you choose to handle through the app, such as CSV files

2. How Your Information Is Used

LogLift uses your information only to provide app functionality, including:

  • Saving and displaying your workouts and workout history
  • Tracking progress, volume, and personal records
  • Remembering your app settings and workout preferences
  • Importing or exporting workout data when you request those actions

LogLift does not use your workout data to build advertising profiles.

3. Data Storage and Processing

LogLift is built as an offline-first app. Workout data and settings are stored locally on your device using on-device storage technologies such as SQLite and local app storage.

At this time, LogLift does not require account creation and does not provide cloud sync. Your data generally remains on your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself.

4. File Access and Sharing

LogLift may allow you to import and export workout data files.

  • If you import a CSV file, the file is processed so the app can read workout data you selected
  • If you export workout data, the app generates a file for you to save or share using your device's available options
  • Files are only handled when you initiate those actions

5. Notifications and Device Features

LogLift includes workout-related features such as rest timer preferences and notification-related settings. If notifications are enabled in the app and on your device, they are used only to support workout functionality.

LogLift does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or location for core app functionality based on the current app version.

6. Third-Party Services

Based on the current version of LogLift:

  • No account system is required
  • No third-party advertising SDK is included
  • No third-party analytics or user behavior tracking SDK is intentionally included

If future versions add services such as subscriptions, analytics, crash reporting, or cloud backup, this Privacy Policy should be updated before or at the time those features are launched.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because LogLift stores data on your device, your workout data is generally retained until you delete it, clear the app's data, uninstall the app, or use any in-app delete or reset features that may be available.

Exported files are managed by you after export. You can delete those files from your device or storage provider at any time.

8. Children's Privacy

LogLift is not directed to children under 13, and the app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

LogLift uses reasonable measures available within the app's architecture to store data locally on your device. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and you are responsible for securing access to your device and any files you export.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If the policy changes, the updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
