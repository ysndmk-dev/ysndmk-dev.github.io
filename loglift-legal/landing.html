How it works

From opening the app to finishing the session, the flow stays simple.

  • Start fast: jump into a workout from today, from a saved routine, or from an empty session.
  • Log set by set: save reps, weight, and rest timing without leaving the main workout flow.
  • Track what matters: see duration, total volume, and personal record cues while you train.
  • Review later: revisit workout history to spot trends and stay consistent over time.
