Restore Purchases

1
Open SpendTrace
Launch the SpendTrace application on your device.
2
Go to Profile
Tap on the Profile icon in the bottom navigation bar.
3
Access Subscription Settings
Tap on "Manage Subscription" or "Upgrade" in the Account section.
4
Tap 'Restore Purchases'
Scroll to the bottom of the subscription screen and tap the "Restore Purchases" link.
Note: If you have an active subscription associated with your Apple ID or Google Play account, your premium features will be instantly reactivated. No duplicate charges will occur.
